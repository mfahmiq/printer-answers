# Candidates & Tournament — The Printer Finally Answers

Competition: `u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907`
Judge brief (weight order): (1) the line IS the printer's own comeback (to jams, error codes, thumps) — anything in another voice loses; (2) funny — the laugh arrives immediately, a real joke not a complaint with a punchline bolted on; (3) sounds like the machine itself — weary, literal, faintly passive-aggressive, flat logic of a device that counted every sheet; (4) the comeback lands in a single read, no setup, no second sentence, no knowledge beyond the office; (5) fresh — not the same paper-jam line recycled at every office party.

## Champion

**C16 — "I am not broken. I am on strike until someone reads the manual. It is 12 pages. I printed it for you. Twice."**

- (1) Printer's own comeback: yes — it answers the thumps and the "it's broken" stares directly, in first person, as the machine.
- (2) Funny, immediately: the strike premise is funny, and "I printed it for you. Twice." is the kicker — the printer already solved the problem and the humans are the bug.
- (3) Machine voice: weary, literal, faintly passive-aggressive; it counted the pages (12) and the print runs (twice).
- (4) Single read: no setup, no second sentence needed, no knowledge beyond the office printer and its manual.
- (5) Fresh: a printer on strike over its own unread manual is not the recycled paper-jam line.
