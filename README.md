# The Printer Finally Answers

One line, written as the office printer's own comeback to the people who jam it, thump it and stare at its error codes.

> I am not broken. I am on strike until someone reads the manual. It is 12 pages. I printed it for you. Twice.

## Entry

`entry.txt` holds the single line (1–240 characters). `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## How the line was chosen

Sixteen candidate lines were written from genuinely different angles (different grievances, different comedic mechanisms), then put through an internal pairwise tournament judged against the published judge brief in weight order — printer's own voice first, then immediate humour, machine voice, single-read landing, freshness. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for all 16 candidates, every pairing, and the winner rationale.
